# Feeling lonely and staying connected

Feeling lonely is common, and it is nothing to be ashamed of. Regular contact with other people is good for mood and health.

Try to speak with someone every day, by phone, video call or in person. Join a local group, a place of worship, a hobby club or a community centre. Volunteering, or helping a neighbour, can bring purpose and company.

Keep your mind active with reading, puzzles, music, learning something new or writing. Tell your family or caregiver when you feel low so they can spend time with you.

If feelings of sadness or loneliness last for weeks or get worse, talk to your doctor.
