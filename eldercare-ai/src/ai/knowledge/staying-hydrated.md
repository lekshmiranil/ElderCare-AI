# Staying hydrated

Older people often feel less thirsty, so it helps to drink fluids regularly through the day rather than waiting until you are thirsty. Keep a glass or bottle of water where you sit.

Water, soups, buttermilk, coconut water and fruit with a lot of water in them all count. Take extra care in hot weather, when you are unwell with diarrhoea or vomiting, or when you are very active.

Dark yellow urine, a dry mouth, headache or feeling dizzy and tired can be signs that you need more fluids. If a doctor has told you to limit fluids, follow their advice.
