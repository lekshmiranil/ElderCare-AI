# Scam calls and staying safe online

Be careful with calls, messages or emails that rush you or say there is a problem with your bank account, a prize or a delivery. Real banks and officials do not ask for your PIN, password or one-time code.

Never share an OTP, PIN, password or card number with anyone who contacts you. Do not install apps or click links because someone told you to. Hang up and call the organisation back on a number you already trust.

If you are unsure, stop and ask a family member or your caregiver before you do anything. If you think you have been tricked, tell your bank and your family straight away.
