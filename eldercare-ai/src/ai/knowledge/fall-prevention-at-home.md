# Fall prevention at home

Good lighting helps you see where you are walking. Keep a light or torch near your bed, and use night lights on the way to the bathroom.

Remove things you can trip over, such as loose rugs, trailing wires and clutter on the floor. Keep walkways clear and keep everyday items within easy reach so you do not need to climb or stretch.

Wear well-fitting shoes with a firm sole indoors and outdoors. Slippery socks and loose slippers make slips more likely. A non-slip mat in the bathroom and a sturdy handrail near the toilet and shower can make moving around safer.

Stand up slowly after sitting or lying down. If you feel dizzy, sit back down and wait before you try again. If you fall or often feel unsteady, tell your caregiver or doctor.
