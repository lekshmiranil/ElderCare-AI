# Staying active

Regular gentle movement helps strength, balance, mood and sleep. Walking, stretching, gentle yoga, tai chi, gardening and dancing are all good options.

Start slowly and build up. Choose activities you enjoy, and do them with a friend or family member if you can. Sit-to-stand exercises from a sturdy chair help leg strength.

Warm up first, wear comfortable shoes, drink water, and stop if you feel chest pain, severe breathlessness or dizziness. Ask your doctor before starting a new type of exercise if you have a health condition.
