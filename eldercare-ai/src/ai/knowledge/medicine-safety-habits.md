# Medicine safety habits

Keep an up-to-date list of all your medicines and show it to your doctor or pharmacist at every visit. Ask them if you are unsure what a medicine is for.

A weekly pill organiser, set up with help from your pharmacist or caregiver, can make it easier to remember what to take. Link medicine times to daily routines such as breakfast or brushing your teeth, and use reminders.

Take medicines exactly as prescribed. Do not stop, skip, double or change a medicine because you feel better or worse. Always talk to your doctor or pharmacist first.

Store medicines in a cool, dry place away from children, and check expiry dates. If you miss a medicine, do not guess what to do. Ask your pharmacist or doctor.
