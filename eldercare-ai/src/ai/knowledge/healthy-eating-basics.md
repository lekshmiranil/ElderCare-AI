# Healthy eating basics

Eat regular meals with a variety of foods: vegetables, fruit, pulses, whole grains, and some protein such as dal, eggs, fish or yoghurt. Smaller, more frequent meals can help if your appetite is small.

Go easy on very salty, sugary and fried foods. Include foods with fibre to help digestion, and drink enough fluids.

If you have a health condition such as diabetes, high blood pressure or kidney problems, follow the food advice your doctor or dietitian gave you. If you lose weight without trying, or have trouble chewing or swallowing, tell your doctor.
