# Staying safe in hot weather

Older bodies cope less well with heat. Stay indoors in the hottest part of the day, keep rooms cool with fans, curtains or shade, and wear light, loose cotton clothes.

Drink fluids regularly and take cool showers if you can. Avoid heavy exercise outdoors in the heat, and wear a hat or carry an umbrella when you go out.

Check on neighbours or relatives who live alone. Headache, confusion, very hot dry skin, a fast pulse or feeling faint can be signs of heat illness. Move to a cool place, sip water, and get medical help urgently if the person is confused or unwell.
