# Sleeping better

Try to go to bed and wake up at about the same time every day, including weekends. A regular routine helps your body know when to sleep.

Get daylight in the morning and keep active during the day. Short naps early in the afternoon are fine, but long or late naps can make it harder to sleep at night.

Avoid tea, coffee and cola in the evening, and avoid heavy meals close to bedtime. Keep your bedroom dark, quiet and comfortable, and switch off bright screens for a while before bed.

If you often cannot sleep, wake up very tired, or snore loudly and stop breathing for moments, speak to your doctor.
