# Keeping your mind active

Using your mind helps keep it sharp. Read, do crosswords or number puzzles, play card or board games, learn a new skill, listen to music or write about your memories.

Stay in touch with people and join conversations. Regular physical activity, good sleep and healthy food also support your thinking and memory.

Using notes, calendars and reminders is a sensible habit at any age. If you notice that memory problems are getting in the way of daily life, speak to your doctor.
